I am a doctoral researcher at the Berlin Social Science Center (WZB) in the department of Political Economy of Development and reasearch group on [Institutions and Political Inequality](https://www.wzb.eu/en/research/political-economy-of-development/institutions-and-political-inequality). I am also a member of the Research Training Group ["Dynamics of Demography, Democratic Processes and Public Policy"](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/dynamics/about-dynamics), jointly run by the Hertie School and Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. I hold a MSc. in Economics and Management Science from Humboldt University and a BSc in Liberal Arts and Sciences with a focus on Politics, Philosophy, and Economics from Erasmus University Rotterdam.  

My research interests include public goods, collective action, urban development and vertical social cohesion. My research endeavors to identify success cases of policy innovation and challenge the assumptions that underlie “best practices” promoted by the international development community and to generate insights that inform both policy and practice. Methodologically, I use a diverse set of quantitative and qualitative tools for data collection and analysis such as causal inference, experiments, original surveys, interviews and fieldwork. My current projects are based in Lebanon, Nigeria and Zimbabwe.

Before joining the wZB, I worked as a research fellow and project coordinator at the [Africa Policy Research Institute](https://afripoli.org/), where I conducted research on sovereign debt, digital governance, and credit ratings across African countries, and coordinated high-level policy dialogues. I also have professional experience with the [International Alumni Centre](https://www.iac-berlin.org/), cultural and research collectives in Berlin, and has co-founded the [Isusu Ffena Collective](https://www.instagram.com/isusu_ffena/).
